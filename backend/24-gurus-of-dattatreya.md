# The 24 gurus of Dattātreya

The teachings are in [24-gurus-teachings.json](24-gurus-teachings.json). This page only says what is in it and where.

Each of the 24 gurus Dattātreya names to King Yadu (Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 11.7.33-35), plus the body as an extra teacher (the `+` row), has statements from three sants. Every statement carries its sant, guru, source and, for the translations, the original text.

## What to know before using it

- **Prabhupāda** — verbatim from vedabase.io: each verse's translation and each purport paragraph, nothing reworded. The text is © The Bhaktivedanta Book Trust International, so publishing it needs their permission. Canto 11 is published under Prabhupāda's name, but ISKCON-affiliated sources say it was completed after 1977 by Hridayananda Dasa Goswami with Gopiparanadhana Dasa; check with the BBT before calling these his own words.
- **Eknath and Tembe** — the English is our own machine translation of selected ovis, marked `needs_review`; the Marathi is kept beside it. Only Tembe's adhyay 50 was audited line by line. Tembe's text was recovered from a PDF in a legacy font, so a stray letter may be wrong.
- **The body** is not one of the 24; the avadhūta takes it as a further teacher.
- Tembe numbers the last four gurus differently (serpent 21, arrow-maker 22, wasp 23, spider 24); the file follows the Bhāgavatam.
- Both Eknath and Tembe speak as male renunciates and are often harsh about women; the lesson-bearing lines are kept and a few insulting ones left out. `meta.notes` in the JSON lists what was skipped.

Sources: [Vedabase ŚB 11.7-9](https://vedabase.io/en/library/sb/11/), [Eknathi Bhagavat on santeknath.org](https://santeknath.org/adhyay-7.html), [Datta Mahatmya PDF](https://www.dattamaharaj.com/sites/default/files/2025-Downloadable_file/Dattamahatmya.pdf).
