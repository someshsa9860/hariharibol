# Bhagavad-gītā: characters per chapter

Counted from the `speaker` field of the Sanskrit files
(`backend/.fill-work/source/json/bhagavat-gita/sa/`, the local copy of the translation runs' output),
and checked against the "X said" lead-ins in the English translation.

A **character** is someone who speaks a verse's meaning. Named people who do not speak
(for example Bhīṣma blowing his conch in 1.12, or Droṇa) are not counted.
Verse numbers below are within the chapter.

**Correction to the data:** verses 1.3–1.11 are Duryodhana's words to his teacher Droṇa.
The Sanskrit files tag them as Sañjaya, because the speech has no `uvāca` of its own.
The counts here use Duryodhana for those verses. The fix belongs in the batch's cast file,
not in the Sanskrit files, which other books and the app read.

- No female character speaks in the Gītā.
- No verse has a speaker change in the middle. Each verse has one speaker.
